FalconLens – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

FalconLens – Screen Magnifier is a screen magnifier for Windows. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use it. In short: nothing leaves your computer.

Data we collect

None. FalconLens does not collect, store, send or share any personal information, usage statistics, crash reports or screen content. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

Internet access

FalconLens does not connect to the internet. All magnification happens locally on your computer, in memory, and the magnified image is shown only in the FalconLens window.

Files on your computer

  • Settings (window size and position, zoom, display mode, language) are saved in %APPDATA%\FalconLens\settings.ini on your computer. You can delete this file at any time.
  • Saved views: when you press Ctrl+S, the current magnified view is saved as an image in your Pictures folder. This happens only when you ask for it, and the image stays on your computer.

Microsoft Store

Purchases and downloads are handled by Microsoft Store. Any information Microsoft collects for that is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement, not by this policy. We receive no personal details about buyers.

Children

Because FalconLens collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If a future version ever changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
